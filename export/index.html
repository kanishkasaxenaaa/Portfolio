Vidyalaya Circular Hub, sustainable service design

Giving school uniforms a second term

Families buy new uniforms every year while outgrown ones sit in cupboards. I designed a school-linked reuse service where parents donate and reserve, the school hub cleans and checks, and local tailors repair.

  • One app with three roles: parent, school hub, tailor
  • Usability tested with each role
  • Built around SDG 12, responsible consumption

Pause, inclusive and accessible design

Designing for invisible pain

Period pain is common, under-believed and hard to describe in the moment. Pause offers real-time support that still works when someone can barely look at a screen.

  • Accessibility-first interaction patterns
  • Calm, low-effort flows for high-pain moments
  • Support that reaches the people around them

Nexlin, service design for hotels

Keeping hotel linen connected

A bedsheet changes hands five times before it reaches a guest again, and context drops at every step. Nexlin keeps the item, its status and the person responsible together.

  • Research with five hotel roles
  • Mapped every handoff from room to laundry and back
  • Prototype tested with housekeeping staff
Read the case study

Fintra, behavioural design

Building money confidence in young adults

Many young adults feel unsure about managing money. Fintra is a learning experience that uses behavioural design and small wins instead of lectures.

  • UX research into how young adults handle money
  • Behavioural nudges like spending limits and a pause before paying
  • Gamified, inclusive learning

From canvas to interfaces

I started as an artist, and I still sketch before I open Figma. Art taught me to notice what people feel; design gave me a way to do something about it.

I like messy, real-world problems with many people involved. I do the research myself, map the system, then prototype until it works, often in code with AI tools rather than stopping at the Figma file.

Tools I use most: Figma, Figma Make, Framer, Claude, ChatGPT, Lovable.
