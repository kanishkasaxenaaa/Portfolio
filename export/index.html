About Me

Designing with
intention &
empathy

My journey into design began with sketchbooks, painting, and a love for visual storytelling. From Applied Arts to visual communication, branding, illustration, and freelance design, I've always been drawn to creating experiences that make people feel something.

Over time, that curiosity evolved into UX design — where creativity meets problem-solving, emotion meets interaction, and storytelling meets human behavior.

Currently pursuing my Master's in User Experience Design at the Symbiosis Institute of Design, I explore how thoughtful design can create digital experiences that feel intuitive, meaningful, and human.
