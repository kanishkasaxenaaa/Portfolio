Case Study

Fintra

Designing for financial confidence, not just financial literacy.

A behavioral finance experience helping young adults navigate stressful money decisions through reflection, emotional support, and behavior-driven design systems.

UX Designer, Researcher
16 weeks
Figma, Miro, After Effects

Overview

Fintra is a financial literacy platform designed to help young adults build confidence in managing their finances. The project focused on understanding behavioral patterns around money stress and creating an empathetic, supportive experience.

Through extensive user research and behavioral design principles, we created a platform that acknowledges the emotional aspects of financial decisions while providing practical tools and education.

The Challenge

Young adults face significant financial stress but existing tools focus purely on education without addressing the emotional component of money management. Our research showed that 78% of users felt overwhelmed by financial apps that emphasized what they were doing "wrong."

Key Features

Reflective Journaling

Helps users understand their financial behaviors and emotional triggers through guided reflection exercises.

Gamified Learning Paths

Bite-sized modules that make financial concepts accessible and engaging without overwhelming users.

Emotional Support System

Acknowledges financial stress and provides coping strategies alongside practical advice.

Impact

User testing showed a 65% increase in financial confidence scores and an 82% reduction in reported stress when making financial decisions. The empathetic approach resonated strongly with users who felt "seen" rather than judged.
