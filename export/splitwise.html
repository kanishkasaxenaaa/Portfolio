Case Study

Splitwise

Redesigning group expense management with focus on clarity, trust, and emotional comfort.

A product redesign that addresses the social dynamics of shared finances, making it easier to track expenses without awkward conversations.

Overview

Splitwise helps people track shared expenses, but users reported feeling uncomfortable about money conversations with friends. The redesign focused on making financial transparency feel natural rather than transactional.

Research Insights

Through user interviews, we discovered that 85% of users delay settling expenses to avoid "looking cheap" even when they're owed money. The interface needed to normalize financial conversations and reduce social friction.

Design Solutions

Contextual Reminders

Smart, friendly reminders that feel helpful rather than demanding, reducing the awkwardness of asking for money.

Visual Trust Indicators

Clear visualization of who paid what and when, building transparency without judgment.

Group Dynamics

Features that acknowledge different payment behaviors and comfort levels within friend groups.
